What WatchSentry is

WatchSentry is a free Chrome extension that displays estimated fair-value data on watch listings at supported marketplaces (Chrono24, eBay, and Watchfinder). The displayed values are the median of active public eBay listings (asking prices) over a rolling 90-day window, filtered to comparable conditions, and are presented as informational estimates only. They are NOT appraisals, sold-price valuations, or financial advice.
